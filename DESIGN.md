# ReTryp: Design System

This document captures the visual identity and styling rules of ReTryp. It serves as the baseline for all future design iterations.

## Typography
- **Primary Body Font:** Manrope (Clean, legible, modern geometric sans)
- **Primary Display Font:** Playfair Display (Confident, editorial, premium serif)
- **Tabular Numerals:** Used explicitly for times, durations, and currencies (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Color Palette
- **Surfaces:** Clean off-white and cool grays (`var(--surface-page)`, `var(--surface-card)`).
- **Accents:** Deep Teal (`var(--accent-600)`), chosen for its calming, authoritative presence.
- **Status Colors:** 
  - Never used alone; always paired with a Lucide icon and text label.
  - Red for Disrupted, Green for On-Time, Blue for Recovery, Amber for Risk.

## Mood & Imagery
- **First-Class Lounge Aesthetic:** The application feels expensive because it is exact.
- **Imagery:** Image-led in "Persuade" mode (like the Auth pages), featuring calm, high-end photography (e.g., Unsplash architectural or lounge imagery) with deep gradients for legibility.
- **No Glassmorphism:** Strict adherence to solid, accessible contrasts without glowing or frosted glass effects.
- **No "AI" Sparkles:** The engine is invisible.
